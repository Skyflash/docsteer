DocSteer **1.0** is out. It is a fast, framework-free Jekyll theme for
documentation, support portals and internal knowledge bases.

## What's in it

- Six colour skins (`aqua`, `violet`, `mint`, `ember`, `graphite`, `clay`),
  each with light and dark variants and a live switcher.
- Client-side live search with a keyboard-driven modal.
- Image lightbox with galleries and captions.
- Auto table of contents with scroll-spy, collapsible sidebar, prev/next pager.
- `jekyll-seo-tag`, sitemap, RSS, Open Graph and JSON-LD out of the box.
- One `docsteer:` config block; colour tokens in a single Sass map.
